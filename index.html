What to try next

  1. Post fewer reels and put more into each one

    Two or three good reels a week performed the best, too many reels causes the algorithm to showcase MoMU reels less.

  2. Keep reels quick and simple

    Both of our best reels were short and funny, nothing heavily edited or choreographed, M team showing their humor is the best.

  3. Use carousels for posts

    They get more than double the likes of single photo posts. Recaps, guides and reveals all work well as a swipe, use one whenever there is more than one thing to show.

  4. Aim for midweek

    Tuesday to Friday has done better than Monday or the weekend, but not by much.

  5. Ask people to follow, in the reel

    Lots of people who see our reels do not follow us, a simple "follow @momuians for more" on screen and in the caption helps.
